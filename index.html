How It Works

See who's there.
Always.

01

Passive Recognition

D-Vision glasses scan faces in real time using edge-based AI — no cloud delay, no privacy compromise. Recognition happens in under two seconds.

02

Contextual Display

A subtle HUD shows the person's name, their relationship, and a warm memory prompt — enough to restore confidence, nothing that overwhelms.

03

Audio Guidance

Optional discreet audio cues provide spoken guidance, helping patients engage naturally — even when looking away from the display.

Built for Real Life

Technology that disappears into care.

Real-Time Face Recognition

On-device AI identifies familiar faces in under 2 seconds, even in low light or partial occlusion. No internet required.

Privacy-First Architecture

All recognition happens on-device. No faces, names, or memories are ever transmitted to external servers — ever.

Caregiver Family App

Family members add themselves, upload photos, and write memory prompts — building a recognition library with love.

All-Day Wearability

Designed to look like standard eyewear. 14-hour battery. Lightweight enough that patients forget they're wearing them.
