Built on Trust

The questions that matter most.

Where do the faces live?

On the glasses — nowhere else. Faces and names stay on-device. They never touch a cloud, never leave the frames, and never train anyone else's system.

How hard is it to set up?

A caregiver adds the people who matter in the companion app — just a photo and a name. The person wearing Vela does nothing at all.

What if it doesn't recognize someone?

Vela only ever shows people a caregiver has added. It never guesses at strangers, and when it isn't sure, it stays quiet — so it never puts the wrong name on a face.
