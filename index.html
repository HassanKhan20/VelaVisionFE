How It Works

See who's there. Always.

01

Passive Recognition

The glasses quietly scan for familiar faces — no buttons, no setup, no cloud. Recognition happens on-device in under two seconds.

02

Contextual Display

A gentle display shows the person's name, their relationship to you, and a warm memory — enough to restore confidence, nothing that overwhelms.

03

Voice Companion

A calm, familiar voice through the glasses offers spoken context, gentle reminders, and daily orientation — warm, patient, always there.

Built for Real Life

Technology that disappears into care.

Real-Time Face Recognition

On-device recognition identifies familiar faces in under 2 seconds, even in low light. No internet required.

Privacy-First Architecture

All recognition happens on-device. No faces, names, or memories are ever transmitted to external servers.

Caregiver Family App

Family members add themselves, upload photos, and write memory prompts — building a recognition library with love.

All-Day Wearability

Designed to look like standard eyewear. 14-hour battery. At just 40g, patients forget they're wearing them.
